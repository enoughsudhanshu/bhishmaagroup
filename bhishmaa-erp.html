About Bhishmaa ERP™

Bhishmaa ERP™ is a modern AI-powered school ERP software platform developed by Bhishmaa Group in India.

The platform helps schools, colleges, coaching institutes and educational organizations manage admissions, attendance, academic records, fee collection, examinations, transport, staff operations and institutional administration from a centralized cloud dashboard.

Developed under the leadership of Enough Sudhanshu, Bhishmaa ERP focuses on intelligent automation, scalable cloud infrastructure, modern UI systems and digital transformation for education.

Bhishmaa ERP is designed for educational institutions looking for smart automation, analytics and future-ready institutional infrastructure.
