Bhishmaa Group was founded by Enough Sudhanshu with a singular vision — to bridge the gap between complex enterprise software and the businesses that need it most, without the enterprise price tag or complexity.

Combining deep expertise in AI/ML, Python, Flask, cloud architecture, and automation engineering, every product at Bhishmaa Group is crafted from first principles — purpose-built, not patched together.
