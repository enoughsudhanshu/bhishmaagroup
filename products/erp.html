Bhishmaa ERP

A Centralized Platform for Modern Multi-School Administration

Managing modern educational networks demands efficiency, accuracy, and clear communication. Bhishmaa ERP is a comprehensive, multi-school management solution designed to streamline academic records, administration, staff coordination, and parent-student engagement across multiple branches from a single unified portal.

Bhishmaa ERP Overview

Bhishmaa ERP focuses on removing administrative roadblocks by organizing educational processes into functional workflows. From entry validation to outcome reports, all data resides in a highly secure environment.

Development Focus Scalable Infrastructure
Primary Audience Educational Institutions

Key Features Built For School Groups

Bhishmaa ERP includes built-in operational modules to coordinate and execute daily administrative duties effectively.

Student Management

Track and maintain student life-cycle profiles, academic histories, emergency contacts, and documentation centrally.

Attendance Management

Monitor and log real-time student and staff attendance status. Automate reports and notifications for absences.

Fee Management

Automate class billing cycles, collect online payments safely, allocate installments, and audit outstanding receipts easily.

Examination Management

Design exam schedules, configure marking schemes, record invigilation, and manage exam execution logs.

Result Processing

Compile marks, evaluate grades automatically based on institutional guidelines, and generate print-ready report cards.

Staff Management

Manage teaching and administrative personnel profiles, department alignments, scheduling, and portal roles.

Parent Access

A secure dedicated login portal for parents to view academic progress, fee receipts, alerts, and report cards.

Academic Records

Archive historical logs, transfer certificates, student behavior markers, and institutional logs securely.

Reports and Analytics

Synthesize operational data into simple visual charts. Audit fee collection, attendance records, and branch metrics.

Multi-School Support

Manage multiple institutions, campuses, or branches from a super-administrator dashboard with fine-grained access control.

Core Operational Benefits

Secure Multi-Campus Isolation

Campus data is segmented and protected, ensuring local branch staff only access relevant school records, while group admins maintain bird's-eye views.

Reduced Paperwork & Manual Errors

Consolidating grade inputting, attendance logs, and bill generation reduces the time spent on manual database entry and updates.

Instant Stakeholder Communication

Ensure administrative announcements, circulars, fee due alerts, and student performance summaries reach parents directly through safe login accesses.

Common ERP Use Cases

  • Group Schools Administration Corporate entities or chains managing multiple public/private schools under a single board.
  • Campus Branches Synchronization Independent campuses needing standardized examination processing, grading rules, and unified parent messaging.
  • Centralized Fee & Financial Auditing Financial managers seeking to aggregate fee collection reports and outstanding lists across branches.
