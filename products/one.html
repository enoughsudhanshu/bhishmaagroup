Bhishmaa One

Multi-Business Billing & Inventory Operations System

Running multiple trade channels or stores calls for standard, simplified tracking methods. Bhishmaa One is an integrated business management platform that unifies billing, inventory levels, buyer accounts, and sales pipelines into a central repository, allowing operators to monitor multi-company health in one dashboard.

Bhishmaa One Overview

Bhishmaa One organizes trade details, from purchase tracking to final customer invoicing, into standardized audit reports. It accommodates multi-tenant operations securely.

Engineered for Operational Performance

Explore the tools built inside Bhishmaa One to keep business files organized, current, and accessible.

Standard Billing

Construct invoices instantly. Choose items from registered inventories, apply discounts, and export print-ready PDFs.

GST Ready Templates

Calculate local and interstate taxes (CGST, SGST, IGST) automatically based on standard tax slabs and HSN/SAC codes.

Inventory Tracking

Monitor stock levels in real time. Configure threshold warnings to receive notifications when stock counts drop low.

Sales Tracking

Record all point-of-sale data automatically. Classify sales logs by business department, date, or clerk.

Purchase Monitoring

Log vendor bills, supply acquisitions, and shipping expenses to compute the net cost of goods sold accurately.

Customer Catalog

Track customer accounts, historical orders, outstanding credits, contact files, and membership profiles securely.

Business Reports

Compile profit margins, tax summaries, stock valuations, and expense registers into clean, ready-to-present spreadsheets.

Multi-Business Dashboard

Incorporate and govern multiple brands, legal entities, or branch offices from a unified administrative account.

Core Operational Benefits

Centralized Audit Logging

Store all invoices, tax allocations, and purchase registers under a unified database. Keep company files ready for tax audits.

Precise Inventory Valuation

Minimize overstocking costs by mapping stock-out timelines and stock values in real time relative to demand cycles.

Flexible Branch Scaling

Add separate store outlets or service offices to your portal without needing independent database deployments.
